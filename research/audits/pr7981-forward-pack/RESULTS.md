# Verdict and evidence

**Counterexample to the local spending premise for the executed gate sequence.** On head `9585c1fa2ed5f2316ac09e04b357cb720badfcb8`, pack `3x5-P72/left-forward/pack-1/physical-axis-1-side-3` satisfies the intended padded Fourier mean within its output tolerance. The same trace cannot satisfy `GatePacks.GateWitness.spend` at the required tolerance with one stage per executed diagonal scan or H dispatch. The diagnostic explicitly supplies 72 working bits. The production precision formula at this geometry gives 295,157 bits.

## Three consecutive dispatches suffice

Let $q=2^{72}$ be the working denominator, $D=2^{48}$ the public denominator, and $N=23{,}816$ the number of dispatches. The local tolerance in `at_selected_precision` is $\delta=1/(8DK)$ with $K=d+1=3$. Even if one uses the two actual forward packs, `from_gates_to_bits` requires a *single* tolerance for that direction with $2\delta\le1/(8D)$. Thus $1/(16D)$ is the most generous uniform tolerance compatible with the observed count.

1. At stage 20, physical slot 20, the unrounded rational diagonal is $-5/24$. The incoming imaginary word is 36 and the recorded outgoing word is $-7$. The exact imaginary result is $-7.5$ in working-word units. The full incoming field has norm at most $1/16$, checked by squaring all 32 complex words. Therefore the rounded premise forces $\epsilon\ge(1/2q)/(1+1/16)=8/(17q)$.
2. Stages 21 and 22 are a bit-0 Hadamard and the diagonal $(-3,1,-3,1)$. On one fibre, input $(1,1,0,0)$ goes to $(2,0,0,0)$ and then $(-6,0,0,0)$. The interval premise quantifies over all inputs, so $M\ge6$.
3. The pack entry has radius below two. Its synthesis distance is nonnegative, hence $\beta\ge0$ for any witness. Already $NM\epsilon(2+4M)+\beta\ge116103/313594649253062377472>3.7023\times10^{-16}$. This is more than **2.50 times** the $K=3$ allowance and **1.66 times** the two-pack allowance. Run `python -B witness.py pack.json.gz` for the short exact-rational certificate.

The full checker also finds a dyadic-only rounding witness. At stage 26, slot 17, a gain of $1/8$ sends real word 297770416980937738972 to 37221302122617217372. The exact result is half a working unit smaller. Together with the two-stage gain of six this independently suffices under either coefficient convention for that interval.

## Lean premises and runtime evidence

The ideal operator here applies the forward, mean-normalized ternary character on indices 0, 1 and 2 and copies index 3 on each of the eight fibres. The distance is the largest Euclidean complex modulus over all 32 physical slots. The gate states are in working units of $2^{-72}$. The tolerance uses the public grid unit $2^{-48}$.
